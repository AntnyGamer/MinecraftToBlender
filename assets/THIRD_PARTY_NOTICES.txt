MiEx (Minecraft Export) - Bram Stout Productions
https://github.com/BramStoutProductions/MiEx

Minecraft to Blender downloads (and verifies the SHA-256 of) an official MiEx release,
then loads a bundled, modified ChunkBedrock class ahead of the unchanged JAR
for Bedrock exports. The reproducible source patch is in
tools/build_bedrock_reader_patch.py. It fixes block-entity section bounds,
preserves shulker facing, translates Bedrock banner dyes/patterns and normalizes
legacy sign text, potted plants, legacy skull types/rotation and Bedrock armor
stand equipment while retaining the original saved tags.
It also prevents translating already translated palette states a second time,
preserves bed dyes and undyed shulker IDs, and reads saved piston extension.
It resolves legacy chain IDs against available resource definitions and applies
saved chest pair coordinates to normal, trapped and copper chest variants.
Additional BSD MiEx skull, armor-stand and equipment model definitions are
adapted by tools/build_bedrock_detail_models.py. Their inputs are pinned and
the generated JSON files are reproduced in CI.
Copper chest definitions adapt MiEx's BSD chest geometry and UV layout.
Copper statues use Mojang's pinned copper_golem.geo.json standing geometry;
non-standing Bedrock pose metadata is not yet verified. The generated standing
mesh and UVs are compared with MiEx's native Bedrock model reader in CI.
Detail geometry and UV measurements were checked against Mojang client 26.1.2
(SHA1 4e618f09a0c649dde3fdf829df443ce0b8831e65). Static armor-stand pose rotation
data is derived from Mojang/bedrock-samples revision
15701fa9c13923f12662984c32c124327e40e3d3, resource_pack/animations/armor_stand.animation.json.
https://github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples
The EXE does NOT bundle MiEx.jar, Minecraft client JARs, texture images,
world saves, or Blender. Minecraft texture images are installed on demand.
The End portal fallback uses the verified blocks/end_portal.png from the same
pinned Mojang sample revision. Portal surfaces are static texture approximations;
Minecraft's animated portal shader and gateway beams are not reproduced.
The Blender importer and MiEx resource-pack templates are downloaded at run time
from pinned MiEx repository commit 631e9fc2fde8c749bf59d4c3495492e62a83ef6c.
